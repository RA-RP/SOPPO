## Main Technical Track Deadlines

All deadlines are anywhere on earth (UTC-12).

- Abstracts due: 2026-07-21 23:59 UTC-12.
- Full papers due: 2026-07-28 23:59 UTC-12.
- Supplementary material and code due: 2026-07-31 23:59 UTC-12.

## Abstract Submission

At abstract submission time, AAAI27 requires a full paper title and a complete abstract in OpenReview. Placeholder titles or abstracts are deleted and cannot proceed to full paper submission. Paper topics and nominated reciprocal reviewer are frozen after the abstract deadline.

## Paper Submission

For review, only the anonymous PDF is required, but the reproducibility checklist must be completed and uploaded separately at paper submission. The main track limit is 7 pages of main content, maximum 9 pages total, with pages beyond 7 reserved for references. Supplementary material may include a technical supplement PDF, media archive ZIP, and code/data package ZIP.

## Modification Window

Between abstract deadline and full paper deadline, authors and author order can be changed, and the submitted paper can be changed. Title, TL;DR, and abstract can be edited but should not substantively change. After the full-paper deadline and until the supplementary deadline, only supplementary materials can be changed. After the supplementary deadline and before notification, nothing can be changed.
